Reading this view

Colour shows how closely each service depends on your selection.

  • Selected — the service you may change
  • Direct — depends on the selected service
  • Level 2 — depends through one other service
  • Level 3+ — farther transitive dependents
  • Dimmed — no dependency path found

Node size reflects incoming references found by the scanner. Highlighted links are potential impact paths, not proof that a change will break a service.
